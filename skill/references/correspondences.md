## Worked Examples

Each example shows the two candidate planets a statement could plausibly read as, the axis that decides between them, and the runner-up that was set aside.

### "I WILL FINISH THIS BOOK"

- **Candidates:** Mercury (writing — the subject matter is language, a book) vs. Saturn (finishing, completing, holding a line to the end — the verb)
- **Axis:** subject vs. verb. The territory is Mercury's; the change being asked for is Saturn's.
- **Taken:** Saturn — the working is about durability and completion, not about the writing itself.
- **Runner-up:** Mercury, set aside because the statement isn't asking for better or easier writing — it's asking for the thing to actually end.

### "I WILL BE PAID WHAT I AM WORTH"

- **Candidates:** Venus (worth, value, what a person is due — the subject matter) vs. Mars (demand, take, force the confrontation — a possible verb reading)
- **Axis:** subject vs. verb, same as above, but reversed which one wins. Here the change being asked for is establishing a rightful value, not delivering a blow.
- **Taken:** Venus — the working is about worth being recognized and received, not about the confrontation required to get there.
- **Runner-up:** Mars, set aside because the intention isn't phrased as an act of force — it's phrased as a rightful correction of value.

### "I WILL STOP SHRINKING MYSELF"

- **Candidates:** this one doesn't split across two planets — it demonstrates why Saturn's row has to hold both poles at once rather than picking one. Read one way, "stop shrinking" sounds like Mars (force, cutting through a habit). Read the way the working actually asks for it, it's about ending a restrictive structure (the old, small boundary) *and* claiming a stable, durable one (a self that doesn't collapse back down) — the same working, both poles active simultaneously.
- **Axis:** verb-force reading (Mars) vs. structural reading (Saturn), decided by what the intention is actually asking to become durable, not just what it's asking to stop.
- **Taken:** Saturn — holding both poles, restriction ending and stability arriving as one motion.
- **Runner-up:** Mars, set aside because the intention isn't asking for a single sharp cut — it's asking for a new, lasting shape.
